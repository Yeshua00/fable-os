## Downside signals

1. **Growth is being financed by receivables**: the cash-flow statement shows **−19,953** from receivables growth inside the 45,702 of 9M OCF (R7) — i.e. ~44% of reported operating cash was absorbed by customer payment lag. Absolute DSO ≈ 68 days (31,025 ÷ (41,456/91)) vs ≈ 91 days a year ago, so efficiency improved, but a 235% receivable build in one year is the classic top-of-cycle channel-financing signature.
2. **Inventory is 81.2% work-in-process, finished goods nearly empty**: WIP **6,960** of 8,567, finished goods **621 vs 1,094** (−43.2%) (R13). Sold-out is bullish now, but there is almost no finished buffer: any demand air-pocket lands as WIP in a commodity that repriced down 60%+ in prior cycles. At 84.6% GM, this is a peak-cycle margin structure.
3. **Capex commitment is now large in absolute terms**: 19,602 in nine months (24.8% of revenue, R7), supported by **2,989** of government-incentive proceeds — fixed build commitments against a spot-price-driven revenue line.
4. **Unexplained liability jump**: other non-current liabilities went **1,443 → 7,086** (+5,643; +5,203 of it in the 9M cash-flow bridge, R4/R7). The face statements do not explain it; a note-level read (deferred tax / government-incentive obligations) is owed before treating the equity build as unencumbered.
5. **Liquidity perimeter tightened slightly while untouched**: revolver cut **3.50B → 2.00B** on 2026-05-06 with nothing drawn, net-leverage covenant ≤3.25x (R16) — immaterial at net cash 24,406, but it is a direction-of-travel marker.
