## Strategic Rationale (WHY NXP Wants AMBA)

### 1. Edge AI for Automotive (PRIMARY)
- NXP is the #1 automotive semiconductor company by revenue
- AMBA's CVflow AI processors are purpose-built for vision AI in cars (ADAS, autonomous driving, driver monitoring)
- NXP already acquired Kinara (edge AI startup, $307M, Nov 2025) — AMBA would be the second major edge AI acquisition
- **Combined:** NXP's automotive platform + AMBA's vision AI = comprehensive autonomous driving solution

### 2. Computer Vision IP
- AMBA's CVflow architecture is unique — low-power, high-performance video processing
- Patents in video compression, image processing, computer vision
- Complements NXP's MCU/SoC portfolio with dedicated AI inference hardware

### 3. Market Timing
- Edge AI chip market consolidating — smaller players being absorbed
- Automotive AI SAM growing rapidly (NXP cited $22.9B by FY2032 for AMBA's SAM)
- First-mover advantage in integrating vision AI into automotive platforms

### 4. Competitive Response
- Qualcomm acquiring automotive chip capabilities
- NVIDIA dominating data center AI, pushing into automotive
- NXP needs to close the AI gap — AMBA is the best available target
